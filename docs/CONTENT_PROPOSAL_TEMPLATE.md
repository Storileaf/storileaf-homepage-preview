# Public website content proposal and approval record

Copy this template for every substantive public copy proposal. Follow the [content gate](CONTENT_GATE.md).

## Source and audience

Page and section

Section job and intended reader

Current exact copy, with approval state and source link

Proposed exact copy, with alternatives clearly separated

## Product fact verification

Authoritative private main document and commit, including relevant lines

Claim-by-claim verification or statement that the proposal contains no product claims

Any conflicting or unverified assertions and the decision to omit them

## Editorial check

British English and calm, specific brand tone

Reader comprehension and conversion action

Repetition against adjacent sections and copy ledger

Image/alt text, metadata, accessibility and privacy implications

## Destination gate

Exact existing verified URL or unbuilt destination status

No submission, purchase, resource or newsletter behaviour implied without an actual working integration

## Decision and implementation

Founder approval of exact copy (quote the decision and date)

Separate authorisation to implement

Source change or commit reference

Browser QA, mobile screenshots, verified destination and remaining issues

Production-release status (do not assume from preview success)
