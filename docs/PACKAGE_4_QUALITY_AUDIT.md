### Coverage limits and remaining acceptance

Writing used Chrome for Testing 155.0.8059.84, Playwright 1.56.1 and Ubuntu 24.04.5. Initial and final runner images differ while the browser executable and harness hashes match; no permanent measurement-stability conclusion is claimed. Touch was emulated and text enlargement was targeted font/line-height doubling. Native zoom, physical devices, other engines and actual screen-reader speech were not tested here. An accessibility-tree description is not evidence of a spoken announcement.

P4-05's source repair and automated verification are complete. Its original screen-reader criterion is still open, so the whole acceptance row is not marked closed. On the isolated corrected build, record the OS, browser, screen reader and verbosity settings; open Try answering a question by keyboard; listen for the question and privacy description; check the answer field's name/description; traverse the controls; close with Escape and verify trigger focus; then reopen and confirm the answer is empty. Investigate missing or inappropriate announcements rather than inferring success from this tree check. The approved question and privacy wording must not be rewritten as part of that test.

The existing P4-06 enlarged heading observation remains unchanged. Ten description warnings in the final quality report belong to the deliberately unchanged published baseline, not the corrected source. Two further entries record the existing enlarged 320px heading extent, one per version. This is not a broad accessibility certification.
