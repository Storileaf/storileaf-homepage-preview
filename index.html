A few things you might want to know before you begin.

Ask us a question
Do I need to be a writer?

No. Guided questions give you a starting point, so you can work on one memory at a time. You bring your experiences and your own words. StoriLeaf helps prepare them for a finished book.

Can I give this as a gift?

Yes. You choose a book and package, then the recipient is invited to tell their story. The finished book is created after they contribute and review their draft. Giving the experience does not automatically give you access to their private answers.

Explore gifting
How much time will I have to write?

Each package has a stated period for contributing your stories. Choose the time and support that suit you. The time for preparing, reviewing, printing and delivering the book is separate from your writing access.

Compare the packages
Can I add photographs or speak my answers?

Photographs and voice transcription are available in selected packages. Spoken answers are converted into editable text, not an audiobook. The letter book is a written format. Check the inclusions for the book and package you choose.

See what is included
Do I have to answer every question?

No. You can choose the questions that are relevant to the story you want to tell. The aim is a meaningful book, not a record of everything. Review your contributions before submitting them for preparation.

Will I see my book before it is printed?

Yes. You review a prepared draft before the book goes to print. Your package sets the included support and feedback. The process also includes choosing a cover after your draft is ready.
